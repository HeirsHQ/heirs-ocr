Common causes
In our experience, it is almost always one of these three.
The card expired
Replace it with a current card — the saved billing address usually still applies.
Insufficient funds at the time of the charge
Top up and we’ll retry on {{NextRetryAt}}, or trigger a retry yourself from billing.
The bank blocked a recurring charge
Common on corporate cards. Authorising Heirs OCR as a merchant clears it.
